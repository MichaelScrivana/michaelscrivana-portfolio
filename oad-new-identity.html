One A Day · New Brand Identity

Key visuals and photoreal product renders for the One A Day brand relaunch — Wave 1 + 2 across the full portfolio.

Featured Project

The ONE for you.
Every segment. Every format.

I was responsible for all key visuals on the new One A Day brand identity — building the “The ONE for you.” system across Women’s, Men’s, Kids, Prenatal, and 50+ lines. Motion, hundreds of photoreal stills, and channel-ready assets for digital shelf, social, brand .com, and print.

Services Key Visuals, Motion, 3D Product Rendering, Brand Identity Application
Role Lead Designer — Key Visuals

Challenge

One A Day needed a unified visual identity that could scale across dozens of SKUs, two product formats (gummy and tablet), and five distinct consumer segments — each with its own color story, without losing the master brand. Every render had to be channel-ready: sharp enough for a billboard, flexible enough for a mobile PDP tile.

Approach

I built a modular KV system around the new logo lockup and “The ONE for you.” tagline — orange, blue, and cyan brand fields that flex by segment while keeping typography, lighting, and pack architecture consistent. Motion brought the packs to life for social and digital; photoreal stills were art-directed for shelf presence across hundreds of SKU-level deliverables.
